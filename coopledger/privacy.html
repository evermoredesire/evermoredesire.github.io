CoopLedger: Egg & Hen Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

CoopLedger: Egg & Hen Tracker is a backyard chicken egg-tracking app developed by WoowStudios. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. CoopLedger has no user accounts and WoowStudios does not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

Information the app uses

Your flock records, stored on your device only. Your daily egg counts, hen count, feed and egg-sale entries (amounts, dates, and any notes you add), and app settings are saved in the app's local storage on your device. They are never transmitted to us or anyone else, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Sharing a report is your choice. If you tap "Export Monthly Report (CSV)", the app creates a report file on your device and opens your device's share sheet so you can send it wherever you like. Nothing is shared unless you choose a destination in that share sheet.

No other data. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it does not run analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own. The app works offline; an internet connection is only used to load ads.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

CoopLedger: Egg & Hen Tracker is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use data including your device's advertising ID, device information, approximate (coarse) location derived from IP address, and app interaction data. This data is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), and you can opt out of ad personalization there as well.

Permissions

The app requests internet access and network-state access (to load ads), and the advertising ID (used by AdMob, as described above). It requests no other permissions.

Children

CoopLedger: Egg & Hen Tracker is intended for adults who keep backyard poultry (target audience 18+). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
