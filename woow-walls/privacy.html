Woow Walls — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Woow Walls is a wallpaper app developed by WoowStudios. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Woow Walls has no user accounts and WoowStudios does not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

Information the app uses

Favorites, stored on your device only. When you mark a wallpaper as a favorite, that choice is saved in the app's local storage on your device. It is never transmitted to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Wallpaper catalogue. The app downloads its wallpaper catalogue and images from our public hosting (GitHub Pages). As with any web request, the hosting provider's servers may record standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request). We do not use these logs to identify you.

No other data. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it does not run analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Woow Walls is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use data including your device's advertising ID, device information, approximate (coarse) location derived from IP address, and app interaction data. This data is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), and you can opt out of ad personalization there as well.

Permissions

The app requests internet access (to load the catalogue and ads) and the "Set wallpaper" permission (to apply a wallpaper you choose as your home screen, lock screen, or both). Wallpapers you save are written to your device's Pictures folder through Android's standard media storage.

Children

Woow Walls is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
