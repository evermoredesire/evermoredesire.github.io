Canadian Citizenship Test 2026 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Canadian Citizenship Test 2026 is a Canadian citizenship test-preparation app developed by WoowStudios. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Canadian Citizenship Test 2026 has no user accounts and WoowStudios does not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

Information the app uses

Your study progress, stored on your device only. Your answers, missed-questions deck, mock test history, flashcard progress, cheat-sheet unlocks, province selection, and app settings are saved in the app's local storage on your device. They are never transmitted to us or anyone else, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app.

No other data. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it does not run analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own. The question bank is built into the app and works fully offline.

Text-to-speech stays on your device. When you tap Listen, the app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to read questions and answers aloud. The app itself does not record your voice, does not send any audio or text to WoowStudios, and has no server of its own. (Your device's text-to-speech engine is provided by your device manufacturer or Google and is governed by their policies.)

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Canadian Citizenship Test 2026 is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use data including your device's advertising ID, device information, approximate (coarse) location derived from IP address, and app interaction data. This data is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), and you can opt out of ad personalization there as well.

Permissions

The app requests internet access and network-state access (to load ads) and access to your device's advertising ID (used by the AdMob SDK). It requests no other permissions.

Children

Canadian Citizenship Test 2026 is intended for adults preparing for the Canadian citizenship test (target audience 18+). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
