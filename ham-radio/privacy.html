Ham Radio Technician Prep — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Ham Radio Technician Prep is an FCC Amateur Radio Technician exam-preparation app developed by WoowStudios. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Ham Radio Technician Prep has no user accounts and WoowStudios does not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

Information the app uses

Your study progress, stored on your device only. Your answers, learned and mastered questions, mistakes list, bookmarks, practice-test history, lesson progress, streak, cheat-sheet unlocks, and app settings are saved in the app's local storage on your device. They are never transmitted to us or anyone else, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app. The in-app "Reset my progress" option clears your study progress (unlocked cheat sheets and a Remove Ads purchase, if you have one, are kept).

No other data. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it does not run analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own. The question bank is built into the app and works fully offline.

Text-to-speech stays on your device. When you tap Listen, the app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to read questions and answers aloud. The app itself does not record your voice, does not send any audio or text to WoowStudios, and has no server of its own. (Your device's text-to-speech engine is provided by your device manufacturer or Google and is governed by their policies.)

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ham Radio Technician Prep is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use data including your device's advertising ID, device information, approximate (coarse) location derived from IP address, and app interaction data. This data is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), and you can opt out of ad personalization there as well.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play — WoowStudios never receives or stores your payment details. The app keeps only a record, stored on your device, of whether the purchase is owned, so it can turn ads off and unlock the cheat sheets. Purchase history can also be restored through Google Play.

Permissions

The app requests internet access and network-state access (to load ads and to open the exam-session finder links in the Exam-Day Toolkit), the advertising ID (used by AdMob, as described above), and billing (so Google Play can process the optional Remove Ads purchase). It requests no other permissions.

Children

Ham Radio Technician Prep is intended for people preparing for the FCC Amateur Radio Technician license exam (target audience 18+). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
