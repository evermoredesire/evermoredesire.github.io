CDL Prep — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

CDL Prep is a CDL (commercial driver's license) test-preparation app developed by WoowStudios. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. CDL Prep has no user accounts and WoowStudios does not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

Information the app uses

Your study progress, stored on your device only. Your answers, topic scores, missed-question list, exam history, cheat-sheet unlocks, streak, and app settings are saved in the app's local storage on your device. They are never transmitted to us or anyone else, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app or use the in-app "Reset progress" option.

No other data. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it does not run analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own. The question bank is built into the app and works fully offline.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

CDL Prep is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use data including your device's advertising ID, device information, approximate (coarse) location derived from IP address, and app interaction data. This data is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), and you can opt out of ad personalization there as well.

Permissions

The app requests internet access (to load ads) and vibration (for the optional tap feedback you can turn off in Settings). It requests no other permissions.

Children

CDL Prep is intended for adults preparing for a commercial driver's license exam (target audience 18+). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
